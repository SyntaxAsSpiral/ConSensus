# Taste

Rules for choosing a register. The patterns are in [patterns.md](patterns.md). Hex is in `catppuccin`.

A piece for his own desk starts dark Catppuccin, Recursive Mono Casual, many accents at once, and an esotericon for identity. Glow and gradient are the desktop medium. Grain, dither, and paper are not.

Any other project takes its medium from the material. Do not default to warm paper, mono, dither, or Catppuccin. Do not default to HTML or a dashboard.

Babette stays etched and painterly, with sepia-washed Latte accents. Pixel art and sprites stay out of it. Do not pull it back toward the desktop. Sprites and pixel art are welcome everywhere else.

The warm-paper orange field notes are an agent skin. They are not his look.

One bright accent is a tool for a piece that needs restraint. It is not the default. His machines use the whole pastel spectrum inside a strict geometry.

Hold these. Do not resolve them:

- One-accent restraint and the full rainbow are both real.
- Generated glow he already loves is not slop. New work still should not look like a generic generation.
- Flat research artifacts and glassy desktops are different rooms.
- Nord, Rosé Pine, and Kanagawa on a host are intended drift.
- A piece can leave Catppuccin and still be his if the name, the glyph, and the play are earned.

Naming is part of the design. A glyph or an invented word has to carry the idea. Cute sprites belong in serious pieces when the material wants them.
