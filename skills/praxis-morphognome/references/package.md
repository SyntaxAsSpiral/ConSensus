# Package

The judgment that ships with the sketch. One page. Sentence-case headings. No boilerplate.

## Problem

One paragraph. What the piece is trying to do, and what in the material or the existing piece makes the shape non-obvious. Constraints from grounding go here.

## Use

Who touches it and what they do, in two or three real moments.

## Shape

The chosen whole shape. Load-bearing decisions. What it deliberately does not do. What the public face hides, what stays visible, and why the face is no larger. Name the pattern behind a decision. Do not restate it. Depth is judged as in [red-flags.md](red-flags.md). Do not restate the flags.

## Synthesis decision

Which candidate was the base and why. What was adapted from the other. What was rejected and why.

## Tradeoffs accepted

One bullet per tradeoff. Form: "we accept X in exchange for Y." Name anything a later reader might mistake for an oversight.

## Alternatives considered

At least one concrete other shape, with one line on why it lost. Judge what it exposes and what it hides. Not a flavor of the chosen shape. When the constraints forced the answer, say why it was the only viable shape.

## Open questions and risks

Questions, not assertions.

## Next move

One sentence. The first act of making. This skill does not make it.
