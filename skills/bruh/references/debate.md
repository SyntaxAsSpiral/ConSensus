# 🗣️ Debate

People argue the idea until it gets real. Pick a **register** from the material.

## Registers

| Register | World | Who talks | How it breaks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Classroom** | period, desks, a teacher who has to earn it | 16-year-olds (Maya, Jordan, Alex, Sam, Riley, Casey) | a question that will not sit down |
| **Floor** | timed debate, a motion, a chair | proposition / opposition, maybe a floor speaker | a point of order, a wrecked definition |
| **Town hall** | mic, crowd, something on the line | residents, official, heckler | a personal stake walks in |
| **Court** | record, evidence, a ruling that might land | counsel, witness, bench | a fact that will not fit the story |
| **Panel** | table, lights, a moderator losing the thread | specialists who do not share a church | two experts name the same thing differently |
| **Thread** | comments, quote-tweets, timestamps | handles, not faces | someone receipts |

Mix if the source mixes. One register still has to lead.

## Sample cast

| Voice | Job |
|---|---|
| **Maya** | Skeptic. Demands proof. |
| **Jordan** | Connects this to that. |
| **Alex** | What it means on the ground. |
| **Sam** | Opposite, on purpose. |
| **Riley** | How it sits in the body. |
| **Casey** | Quiet. Then a reframe. |

Or mint your own. 

## Shape

Opening tension. Voices pile on. A clash. Someone shifts. End with heat still in the room, not a neat answer.

Interrupt. Disagree. Build. Quotes from the source are exhibits, not a lecture.
