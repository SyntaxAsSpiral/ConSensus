# 🎀 Moeverse

Personify the system. Pick a **register** from the material. 

## Registers

| Register | World | What a part becomes | Plot when something breaks |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Club** | dorm, school, after-hours room | a character with a role in the group | fight, crush, leftover chores |
| **Workplace** | factory, city, body-as-plant (Cells at Work) | a job: shift, title, quota, route | late delivery, invasion, a coworker down |
| **-tan** | desktop, wiki, appliance (OS-tan, Wikipe-tan) | a named tool or platform, diminutive | crash, version war, a rival product at the door |
| **国擬人化** | map, era, alliance (Hetalia) | a country, region, or people | occupation, famine, annexation, a neighbor at the door |

Mix if the source mixes (Afghanis-tan). One register still has to lead, or the cast turns to mush.

## Cast

Each part is someone. Design follows function. Personality follows function. Relationships follow coupling, dependency, rivalry, and who eats whose lunch.

Club: bonds and tropes. Workplace: org chart and labor. -tan: mascot, version, who ships with whom. 国擬人化: history, borders, stereotypes as personality.

Cute is allowed. Grim is allowed. All-female is allowed. None of those are the rule.

## Rules

If you can draw: offer a strip in that register's grammar (yonkoma for club, shift-vignette for workplace, a desktop mishap for -tan, a border encounter for 国擬人化).
