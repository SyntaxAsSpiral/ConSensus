## Stubs

Original lenses, invented for zk. Pick one per need.

- **Field Guide**: a bug, repo or chore as a species entry. Habitat, markings, call, what it's mistaken for, how to approach. *Use for: understanding a thing you keep misidentifying.*
- **Eulogy**: a short, fond obituary for something being deleted, archived or given away. Survived by, cause of retirement, in lieu of flowers. *Use for: letting go without hoarding.*
- **Heist Crew**: the plan as a heist. The mark, the crew (bots as specialists), the vault, the one thing that goes wrong, the getaway. *Use for: multi-bot coordination and sequencing.*
- **Shipping Forecast**: status in the incantatory BBC radio cadence. Region, wind, sea state, visibility, one word for the trend. *Use for: calm fleet/project status when everything feels loud.*
- **Museum Placard**: one object, a tiny label, a curator's note on why it matters and what to notice. *Use for: a single decision or artifact worth slowing down on.*
- **Sprite Sheet**: a task as a pixel RPG enemy or item. HP, weakness, attack pattern, loot drop, XP. *Use for: chores that need to feel beatable.*
- **Mise en Place**: a task as a recipe card. Ingredients and prep, steps, "it's done when," what burns if you walk away. *Use for: starting a physical task.*
- **Estate Tag**: each item or idea gets a sale tag. Condition, provenance, "as is" notes, a price for keeping it. *Use for: triage, keep vs. sell vs. toss.*
- **Haunting Inventory**: unfinished things as ghosts in the rooms they haunt, what each one wants, how to lay it to rest. *Use for: an overwhelming backlog.*
- **Courtroom**: the procrastinated task on trial, with prosecution, defense, one witness, and the verdict as a sentence of one small step. *Use for: calling out stalling with a wink.*
- **Liner Notes**: the day as an album. Tracklist, a featured artist (whichever bot), the hidden track, the producer's note. *Use for: a daily recap or reflection.*
- **Two-Door Adventure**: a choose-your-own-adventure with exactly two doors per page, three pages max. *Use for: decision paralysis.*
